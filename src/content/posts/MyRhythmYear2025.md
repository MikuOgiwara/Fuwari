# Arcade

## Sega

Sega games are so fun to play.

### Maimai

Ah maimai... This year has been marked by this game... a lot lol. Started with difficulty to play 11, and now I can play Master 13. Finished with 13100 rating on Circle.

### Chunithm

Chunithm... is hard for me. I love the game but I never play it, I don't know why lmaoo. Lazy to boot the cabinet I guess? But I am happy with the level I got. I can play 14+, 15 of rating.

### Ongeki

Ongeki was a big surprise this year. Marked by my level which has been revealed?? Finally a game where I am not bad?? Finished with 17.1 of rating and a lot of Master clears.

### Project Diva

I played on a real cab OwO and that's the only thing I did on this game this year lmao.

## Bemani

YEAH... my first introduction to arcade games was through Bemani games. But now... I have a really hard relationship with Bemani games. I don't find any kind of fun when I play them :/

### Sound Voltex

OKAY so. I have cleared every 14 and 16 on EG Final!!! That's cool, finally got my fucking 17 VF after 2 fucking years of being stuck at 16... (I swear I have a big skill issue on SDVX...). Finished the year with 17.130 (thx nabla for the little buff). BUT... yeah, nabla came out finally... but I don't like it and I don't have any kind of fun when I play SDVX. It's sad because it's my first arcade rhythm game. It's just frustrating and tryhard land and I don't like that. So maybe the end of the progress for me? IDK, 2026 will tell us.

### IIDX

OKAY NOW WE TALK. **I. HATE. THIS. SHIT.** The UI sucks, menuing SUCKS, gameplay is just no fun allowed. Only tryhard. I played like 50h this year and it is bad? I have cleared a 10 (and on real cab lol). But I don't have any fun. The song list is so cool but you can't hear it because if you don't have 10000 hours you are bad, and the live mapping (sounds kinda cool on paper) just makes the fact that you can't hear the music. So yeah, 10 clear. Big rage quit. I don't like it and I don't think I will play it more now.

## Other

### Taiko no tatsujin

Finally got a Taiko drum yeah, and holy shit... the stamina lmaooo. I didn't play so much this year, too many games to play lol, but I can clear Oni 8★ and make some 700k scores, it's fineeeee.

### Wacca

I replayed Wacca for the first time after 3 years lol. Game is really cool, I played 3 credits so nothing to say lol.

# PC

And ofc I play a lot of PC rhythm games, but I prefer to play in arcade lol.

## VSRG

### Platina Labs

Discovery of the year. I love this game, it's cute, chill, and the dev team adds new free songs. Hope the game progresses more. I can clear everything in the game but I can SS 23 max lol.

## The O world

You know it, I know it, so let's talk.

### Current state of this shit

After 10 years I finally quit this game. Why? I really don’t like the direction osu! is taking and how osu!lazer is being developed. Ppy ignores taiko/mania/ctb players. The game is really slow to just "work". Things we want/need are still not here, and I know making a game is long and hard... BUT osu!stable was made in less time than lazer has had, and with fewer people :))) And ppy had to do everything. So yeah. I hate lazer. I hate how things evolve. And I hate the community lol. I don't want to talk more about this game, just play GekiChunMai.

### Mania

I don't have a lot of things to say lol. The new mapping sucks lol. I can play 4★ on 4K, 3★ on 6K and 3★ on 7K. No goals achieved, just enjoy songs lol.

### Taiko

After a lot of nerfs I got a 300pp this year and I stopped playing on keyboard for 6 months. Metal maps are cool to play lol.
