# My days in the Android world
I have used Android since Android 2.2... A long time ago... When Apple at this time was a garbage OS lol. But time passed, and now in 2026, you still have the same issues we got in 2011  
- bad long support  
- bad optimisation  
- system inconsistency  
- ads  
- bloatware  

And things became worse since 2024... I am not a fan of Google, I try to avoid all Google services I can, and now Android in 2026... oh fuck... You CAN'T just say "don't use a Google account", you are forced to give all your data to Google... who sell it to make money :>,

## My Apple background
I never really used Apple devices. Before 2024 I NEVER used an Apple device, but in 2024 when I moved with my boyfriend who had a MacBook Air M1, I started to use it AND I loved this laptop, so at this day it was the only Apple product I had used.

# A switch to iOS?
This started back in 2025. AI started to take this world over, and Google looked to really love taking all your data. Plus a major downgrade of Android after so many years... At this time Apple started to make what I found to be a really good OS, the EU forced Apple to enable sideloading, so why not try it?

## How I got forced to switch to iOS
My phone melted.  
No joke.  
It's just DEAD (thanks Nekopara lmao). So I really needed a new phone, BUT I didn't want to spend money on shit stuff. I really wanted to try iOS, iPhones are NOT cheap (phone in generale are not cheap...) so I wanted to try before looking for a new one. My boyfriend had his old iPhone 13 which was in bad physical condition but worked (I'm gonna talk about this later). So let's start the switch.

# Switch to iOS
So as I said, I'm gonna use an iPhone 13.

## Day one
Okay, so I started up the iPhone, started to add my Apple account and... why are you asking me for my Mac password? To explain, this account was created on a Mac for testing something, but was removed and never reused for like 2 years? But I could skip it so let's skip it.

Set my phone in English, and started the Android to iOS transfer... which worked? No errors, nothing. Perfect! And I missed a lot of apps... fuck, it's fine, go to the App Store and redownload them. Why are you asking for my face for a fucking free app? Okay...

After that I wanted to put a wallpaper and why is iOS trying to generate my wallpaper? Okay it's just a basic drawing and iOS doesn't allow me to just set it like I want. It made a weird bug but after 10 tries it was set?

I just saw that iOS put me in a bad location, because putting my phone in English changed a lot compared to Android. How can you just be in France and have another language? iOS doesn't really like it. 


That was a bug on iOS 26.4 but some icons are really blurred.

iOS DOESN'T HAVE AN EQUALIZER.... fuck.

During my Firefox setup, I saw something really annoying. Firefox on iOS doesn't have extensions... so no adblock... And that is SHIT. It's due to an "Apple security policy" (source: Reddit).

## Day two
I HATE FACE ID... it's not a joke and during the time I write this, I still think Face ID IS BAD. It's still slower, doesn't work sometimes for ??? no reason, literally it fails, I turn off the phone and reopen it and it works???

Sometimes it works in weird positions but when I face it directly it doesn't want to unlock. No, I really don't like Face ID.

## Day three
I just saw that my SMS are fucked up. Like they're mixed, I got a group SMS with alllll my contacts, some are just broken? And I read it's a known issue??? Like how the fuck is this fucked? And Apple doesn't allow manually fixing it???? So my only option is to delete some 15-year-old conversations to fix it?? Thanks Apple :).

## Day four
My bank app doesn't allow me to sign in...

## Day five
I just noticed, but my earphones sound really better on this iPhone. It's a really nice pleasure to see it.

During this switch I downgraded from a 120Hz screen to a 60Hz one. Funny thing, my old phone was so bad and slow that it's not a big downgrade. This iPhone is so much more responsive and fast than my old Android... oh and THIS IPHONE IS OLDER... thanks Samsung for having really bad software.

## Day six
iCloud looks like it's full :) Funny, I never allowed it to send all my images to a cloud :) I don't like that it's MY data, let me keep it personally. So I just deleted iCloud and disabled sync.

## Day seven
I got unlogged by Google on my iPhone? For no reason (I still have a Gmail account.)

### End of the first week
It was a really bad start the first days, but after 3 days I started to like iOS and understand how to use it, so I am really confident about keeping it.
